About

Urte Ronja (b. 2001) is a Lithuanian multidisciplinary artist working across painting, ceramics, printmaking, and set design.

Her work explores fantastical worlds and the unseen forces that shape our perception and emotions.

She creates worlds inhabited by beings, and is fascinated by horror, folklore, nature, the macabre, and the occult.
